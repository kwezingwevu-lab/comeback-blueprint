## 2026-11-13 - AI layer, part 2: log by a sentence or a screenshot, the meal check, races that fit the build (LEDGER 36)
- Tell it in a sentence: the quick log on Home has a 16 px box ("Squat 100 for 5, 5, 4 at RPE 8. Weight 88.4. Slept six and a half hours."; the keyboard's dictation works there, and the box grows with a long note). Claude turns it into JSON; the page checks every value against the app's own ranges (`AI_R`: the quick log's weight and sleep, `HEALTH_RANGE` for steps and resting heart rate, the tape form, the Lift grid's kg, reps and RPE, the run log's distance and pace), accepts only lifts the Lift tab shows this week, and shows a preview: valid items with "Leave out", refused items struck through with the reason. "Save N items" writes the same rows the manual forms write, and one Undo takes back what that save wrote. Nothing is written before that tap.
- Sets from a note go under today only, into the place the Lift tab is set to: the preview says "Sets go into your Home Gym log" or "Commercial Gym log" and names each lift as that place names it. A lift listed under two days (lateral raises on Push A and in Extras) is offered under both, the row shows the day it will be filed under, and on a day with no lift session the Extras come first. Weight, sleep, steps, resting heart rate, tapes and runs can be saved up to 14 days back.
- From a screenshot, where the view can send pictures: "Add a screenshot" (a Garmin Connect, Strava or Apple Health screen, or a photo of a notebook). Same preview, same checks. The date the picture states is used if it is a real day in the last 14 days; otherwise no day is filled in and Save waits until he picks one. Hours and minutes become hours on the page (7:32 is 7.5 h); a run Strava already brought in for that day is marked "Already there from Strava" and left out. Garmin cannot pair with a web app; this is the short way round.
- Meal check on Fuel: words, or a photo. A plate is an estimate with its range, and the totals are summed on the page from the items. A printed nutrition table is read, not estimated: the per-serving figures as printed, kJ turned into kcal on the page (÷ 4.184), and a servings field (0.25 to 10; 0,333 works). A reading that adds up to more than one meal says so. "Add to today" writes one row to the new key `cb2_meals` (`{date,t,kcal,p,src,name}`; in `BACKUP_KEYS`, the backup check, `reloadDB`, the vault and the account copy) and Fuel shows "Logged today: X g protein of {target} g, about Y kcal (estimates)". Rows can be removed, with Undo. Today's logged meals still show when Claude is not available, and "How do I hit my protein today?" sees them.
- Races that fit the build (Events): Claude picks at most three by index from the list the page sent; the page drops any index it did not send, ties each pick to the race's name and date as sent, and prints the name, date and distance from its own rows with Claude's one-line reason as text. The card says what was sent, and only when something was.
- "How was my last run?" in Lift's Run section once a run is logged: his last three runs against what was planned those days, and no pace unless his own pace check exists.
- The published capabilities gain `sample:{images:true}`. A picture leaves the page only for the meal check and the screenshot log, and only the one file he picked; the page says so beside each button ("The whole picture is sent, so crop it first if you need to"). Progress photos are never sent. The coach's privacy line now says what goes with a question (a summary of his own log), and the run and fuel questions send only what they need.
- What the reviews changed before this shipped. My first build passed its own 30 checks. A second read of my own tightened five things (an older viewer app with no JSON call would have lost every AI control: the page now reads the JSON itself, `aiParseJSON()`; a model tier is asked for only where the view lists it; a set lands after the last filled row; Undo restores only the lists that save wrote to; plain words about pictures). Then independent reviewers read it under three lenses (the runtime contract, data safety, iPhone use) and audited my fixes: 35 findings over two rounds, a few of them overlapping, all fixed, each with its own check. A verifier then read my fixes and found two slips in them (a phone holding only a meal "restored" its own copy on every open; a debrief was filed under the wrong day pill if he tapped another pill while it streamed); both fixed too. The ones that mattered most:
  - A lift from a parked day (Push B, Pull B, the old Upper day) was accepted and filed where no screen could open it.
  - Back-dated sets could not be edited or removed after the five-second Undo and would have fed the next target. Sets now save under today only (K29 is the way back).
  - A screenshot was lost if the page redrew while the photo picker was open; a date that could not be used was silently replaced by today; "Save" did nothing while another answer was still streaming; a second quick tap on "Read it" landed on Stop.
  - My first fix for a phone that holds only meals would have let that phone write itself over a full account copy. I caught it on a re-read and the audit caught it too. A meal is now written to the account copy, a fuller copy still restores over such a phone, and the meal is kept.
  - An explanation or debrief was thrown away if a log was saved while it streamed. It is kept, with a line saying what it covers.
  - The shared pace formatter could print "5:60/km". Fixed for every screen (`fmtPace`).
